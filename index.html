🛡️ ScamShield AI

Student Digital Safety Assistant

ScamShield AI helps students understand potential warning signs in suspicious messages, job offers, scholarship messages, emails, payment requests, and other digital communication.

How it works

Paste a suspicious message into ScamShield AI. The application identifies potential warning signs, explains why they may be concerning, and provides safer next steps.

The tool is informational and does not guarantee that a message is legitimate or fraudulent.
